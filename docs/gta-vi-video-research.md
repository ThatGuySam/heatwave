# GTA VI video research

Research date: August 30, 2026.

## Short answer

The released material points to a dense modern-Florida crime sandbox. The
clearest design ideas are the contrast between neon city and rougher coastal or
industrial areas, a fixed Jason and Lucia partnership, vehicle-led crime, and a
police system that tracks witnesses and identity.

Heatwave 99 keeps the vehicle-led job and police escape. It replaces simulation
detail with fast movement, hitscan combat, simple pickups, and a compact ray-cast
district.

## What the footage and previews support

- Rockstar names six broad areas: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port
  Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga.
- The setting mixes beaches, clubs, wetlands, faded motels, strip malls,
  industry, backwoods, car culture, local music, wildlife, and social-video
  satire.
- Jason and Lucia are a criminal partnership. The released material shows
  robberies, drug jobs, car theft, heists, shootouts, street racing, and police
  escapes.
- The August 2026 developer preview reports a six-star wanted scale. A witness
  or alarm must report a crime, and police can track face, clothes, vehicle, and
  whether the pair remains together.
- Footage shows quick weapon switching, cover, crouching, shoulder changes,
  tire shooting, and character changes during action.
- Rockstar and firsthand previews describe reactive NPC interactions and
  persistent criminal profiles. They do not confirm unrestricted AI dialogue.
- Basketball, scuba, kayaking, jet skis, fanboats, dirt-bike and stock-car
  racing, gyms, a shooting range, wrestling, fishing, hunting, and club scenes
  appear in official footage or descriptions. A montage shot does not prove
  that every activity has a full progression system.
- Rockstar says the current official footage was captured in game on
  PlayStation 5. Trailer 2 contains equal parts gameplay and cutscenes.

## Claims not used as requirements

The public material does not establish an exact map-size multiplier, an
80-hour campaign, universal interiors, 60 frames per second, a fully
destructible city, procedural social posts, or unrestricted NPC conversations.
The prototype does not rely on those claims.

Leaked footage was excluded.

## Translation into Heatwave 99

The research changed three parts of the prototype.

First, the mission requires a vehicle rather than treating the car as optional
scenery. Second, heat is an escape state with sight checks and a timer, not a
score multiplier. Third, the map puts a bright coastal strip next to faded
blocks and a canal, which preserves the regional contrast at a much smaller
scale.

Everything else stays original. Sunstroke County, Pelican Marina, the coupe,
the gang, the patrol design, the job, the palette, the map, the interface, and
the audio-free presentation belong to this project.
